Debrief

Compare possible responses

Collect a few responses from the group first. Then reveal these options.

Ask: Which response would you choose, and why? What does each one gain? What risk comes with it?
Tip: There is no score. The value comes from comparing tradeoffs and discussing which response best fits the situation.

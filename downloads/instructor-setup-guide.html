Instructor-only · C² Instructor Hub

M3. Communication Styles and Adaptability · Setup guide

New to the hub? Watch the 5-minute walkthrough video on the hub’s launch page (index.html) before your first session.

The Instructor Hub is optional. You can teach from the PowerPoint instead. If you use the hub, you run the whole session from one private console while participants see only the participant display.

1. Before class

  1. Open the hub’s web address and sign in. Use Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Select Open the instructor console.
  3. Slide 1: paste the participant eWorkbook address and the PDF backup link. Slide 38 (or slide 37): paste your evaluation link. The chat messages in the notes fill them in for you; anything still highlighted needs a value before you post.
  4. Slide 3: upload your own “Meet your instructor” slide as a PNG (export it from PowerPoint at 1920 × 1080). Restore the original slide undoes it.
  5. Breakout rooms: if you won’t use breakout rooms, go to slide 32 or 33 and check Use slide 33 (main room, no breakout rooms) instead of slide 32 under This slide. Advance then uses slide 33. Slide 33 is hidden in the PowerPoint for the same reason.

2. Open and share the participant display

  1. Select Open participant display. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for the site and select it again. The top bar changes to Participant display connected.
  2. In Teams or your meeting platform, choose Share, then Window, and pick the participant display window. Don’t share your whole screen; that would show your notes.
  3. Optional: select Enter full screen in the participant display.

The participant display shows only slides, reveals, markup, the blank whiteboard, and the activity timer. It never receives speaker notes or your private notes.

3. One monitor (Windows Snap)

  1. Select Compact layout in the console.
  2. Click the console and press Windows + ←. Click the participant display and press Windows + →.
  3. Collapse the slide menu with « (or press \) for more room. Select » to open it again.
  4. Keep both windows visible. The participant display keeps updating while you work in the console.

4. Two monitors

Keep the console on your main monitor. Drag the participant display to the second monitor and select Enter full screen. Share that window.

6. My notes: private notes on each slide

  • Open My notes at the top of the speaker notes panel. Choose a Type (Reminder, Suggested revision, Content update, Timing, or Technical issue) and type. It saves as you type.
  • Slides with a note show a My note tag in the slide menu. Notes stay in this browser and never appear on the participant display.
  • Print or save as PDF makes a report with each slide picture. Download spreadsheet saves a CSV for the design team. Back up notes and Restore notes move them between computers.

7. Sound

This course has no audio or video. You don’t need to share computer sound.

8. Save your work

Markup, the uploaded slide, and settings stay in this browser. Open Saved work and select Download session file to keep them; Open session file brings them back. Clear session work keeps your settings and your private notes. Back up private notes separately from My notes.

9. If something goes wrong

  • The participant display stopped updating: click into it once, or close it and select Open participant display again. It returns to the current slide.
  • Pop-up blocked: allow pop-ups for the site in the browser’s address bar.
  • The hub won’t load: teach from the backup PowerPoint (Instructor resources → Source PowerPoint (backup)) and the printable notes.
  • Participants can’t open the eWorkbook: post the PDF backup link.

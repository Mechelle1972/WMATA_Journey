Teaching with the Instructor Hub

Transcript of the walkthrough video (5:18). Captions are shown in the video. Times mark the start of each section. The video uses the hub's sample course; every C² course hub works the same way.

0:06 Introduction

The C² Instructor Hub is an optional presentation tool designed to make facilitation easier. If you prefer using your standard PowerPoint and familiar presentation tools, that's perfectly fine. Use the approach that works best for you. This short walkthrough shows you how to teach a live virtual class with the C² Instructor Hub, even if you've never opened it before. We'll use the hub's sample course. Every C² course hub works the same way.

0:31 The two windows

The hub has two windows. The private instructor console is your workspace: every slide, your full speaker notes, and every control. The participant display is a clean window that shows learners only what you choose. Start from the launch page and select Open the instructor console.

0:47 Open the participant display

In the console, select Open participant display. A second window opens and follows everything you do in the console. In that window, select Enable sound and video once, so audio and video can play later. Back in the console, the status changes to Participant display connected.

1:05 The most important rule

Here's the most important rule. Share only the participant display window. Never share your whole screen, because your console shows your notes and the answers.

1:14 One or two monitors

On one monitor, snap the console to the left half of the screen and the participant display to the right half, then select Compact layout. Keep both windows visible, so the shared window keeps updating. With two monitors, move the participant display to the second screen and select Enter full screen. Keep the console in front of you.

1:34 Console tour

Let's tour the console. On the left is the slide menu. Search by number or title, and use the tags to spot reveals, media, whiteboards, eWorkbook activities, and slides you can customize. The center shows exactly what learners see right now. On the right are your private speaker notes, with say, do, and ask cues. Use A minus and A plus to change the text size. Above the slide, the session clock and the time on this slide keep you on pace, and the hub tells you if you're ahead of or behind plan.

2:05 Advance and reveals

To move through the course, use Advance, the right arrow key, or your presenter remote. Like a click in PowerPoint, Advance plays the next reveal first, then moves to the next slide. Answers stay hidden until you reveal them, and the participant display follows each step. Back steps backward, and the build counter shows where you are. Some slides build on their own, just as they do in PowerPoint.

2:28 Collapse the slide menu

Need more room for the slide and your notes? Collapse the slide menu. Select the double arrow at the top of the menu, or press the backslash key. The menu shrinks to a narrow strip that still shows your slide number, and the slide and notes get the extra space. Select it again to bring the menu back. The hub remembers your choice.

2:47 Chat text and the whiteboard

When the notes say paste in chat, select Copy for chat, then paste it into the chat. On whiteboard slides, select Show the whiteboard and type responses from the chat. They appear on the participant display right away. Select Show the slide to go back.

3:03 Audio and video

On a video slide, select Advance once, or Play under This slide. The video plays in the participant display window, and the console tells you it's playing. Pause, Restart, and Stop work the same way for audio. That's why Include sound matters when you share.

3:19 Pointing things out

To point something out, choose a pen, highlighter, or shape and draw right on the slide preview. Learners see your marks. Choose Pointer when you're done, or select Blank whiteboard for an empty page.

3:32 eWorkbook activities

Slides marked eWorkbook with an asterisk have a matching activity in the participant eWorkbook. Learners use their own eWorkbook to take notes beside each slide and complete activities, with a Copy button for sharing answers in chat. Their work stays on their own devices. It isn't sent to you.

3:49 Timers and breaks

To time an activity, set the minutes, add a label if you like, and select Start. Check Show on participant display to put the countdown on screen. On the break slide, type the return time. It replaces the placeholder on the slide.

4:03 Personalize the introduction slide

Before class, make the introduction slide your own. Export your version from PowerPoint as a PNG and upload it here. You can always restore the original.

4:13 My notes

Next: My notes. Open it at the top of your speaker notes to keep a private note on any slide, like a reminder or a suggested revision. It saves as you type, it never appears on the participant display, and the slide gets a My note tag in your menu. When you're done, print all your notes as a PDF, or download a spreadsheet to share with the design team.

4:34 Save your work

Your marks and whiteboard responses stay in this browser. To keep them, open Saved work and select Download session file.

4:43 Before every class

Before every class: open the console, open the participant display, share only that window with sound on, and test it with a colleague. If you get stuck, select Help for the full setup guide. You're ready to teach from one private workspace.

4:58 Closing

And remember, the Instructor Hub is here to support you, not change the way you prefer to teach. Use the tools that work best for you, and focus on what matters most: creating a great learning experience for your participants. Thanks for watching, and happy teaching.

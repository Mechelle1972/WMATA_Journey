Slide 30. A check-in that is not working

Jordan says: These updates are hard to use. I keep losing the decision. Taylor feels criticized. Their next check-in must settle three things: format, timing, and ownership.

Slide 31. Round 1: Draft your response

Paste the instructions.

eWorkbook, Part 5, Round 1 (4 minutes, individual). Write Taylor’s opening response to Jordan in complete sentences. Include: (1) an acknowledgment of Jordan’s concern, (2) one neutral clarifying question, (3) a confirmation of what Jordan needs, and (4) a proposed next step with format, deadline, and owner. Keep your draft. You’ll use it in Round 2. You don’t need to post it.

Slide 32. Round 2: Practice in small groups

Paste the instructions before you open the rooms. Participants won’t see the slide once they’re in their rooms.

Round 2, breakout rooms (7 minutes, groups of 3). TAYLOR reads their Round 1 response. JORDAN starts by saying: These updates are hard to use. I keep losing the decision. Then responds naturally. OBSERVER uses the four-point checklist and names one strength and one improvement. Rotate about every 2 minutes so everyone plays Taylor once. Last minute: revise your own response in eWorkbook Part 5, Round 2. Be ready to post one change you made. Four-point checklist: (1) adapted pace, order, or detail to a stated need; (2) asked a neutral clarifying question; (3) confirmed the other person’s meaning; (4) responded to the feedback and proposed a specific next step.

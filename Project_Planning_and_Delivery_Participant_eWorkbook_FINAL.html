Build the Atlas plan

Produce a planning structure another person can follow.

Build a plan people can execute

15 minutes · Breakout groups
Each plan entry needs all five parts:
  1. Major work.
  2. Milestone or completion point.
  3. Expected timing.
  4. One clear owner.
  5. Resources or support required.

You are building a usable planning structure, not a detailed project schedule.

Workspace section 5

Build stakeholder commitment and accountability

Move from awareness to a specific commitment and a reliable follow-through.

Practice: The commitment slipped

12 minutes · Pairs

Scenario: A team member missed an agreed Atlas deliverable. They say another priority took precedence. The launch window has not moved.

  1. State what was expected.
  2. Confirm what happened.
  3. Name the impact on the project.
  4. Agree on the next commitment.
  5. Clarify the follow-through.

Practice the opening. Keep the focus on the commitment and project impact.

Build the commitment plan

10 minutes · Individual, then main room

Complete the commitment and accountability section, then identify the commitment that will be hardest to get.

Workspace section 6

Atlas control room

Run the check-in, make the call, and write the update.

Keep execution on track

16 minutes · Breakout rooms of four

Scenario: A milestone is behind, and a dependency has not been completed. Launch has not moved.

  1. Decide whether the plan changes or the slip is absorbed.
  2. Name what has to be escalated, and to whom.
  3. Name the stakeholder who needs to hear it first.
  4. Draft the agenda items and the status message.

Bring back: your status message.

State what happened, the impact, the decision, and what happens next.
